Help & contact

Fastest answers

  • FAQ: submissions, scores, appeals and privacy.
  • How grading works: the full method behind every grade.
  • My Data: export or delete your projects and account yourself, instantly.

Contact

Sign in with GitHub and open the Chat / Support button in the bottom-right corner for:

  • Problems or bugs: what you did, what you expected, and the repository link if relevant.
  • Grading mistakes: the fastest route is Report an error in this review on your project page (see how it works). Email for anything that doesn't fit there.
  • Privacy requests or questions: no reason needed.
  • Security issues: please report privately and give a reasonable chance to fix before disclosing.

Appeals about the vibe-coded check are made from the rejected submission itself; see appeals.
