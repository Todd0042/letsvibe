Remove my project

Your work, your choice. You can take any of your projects off LetsVibe, or delete your account entirely, at any time. No questions, no waiting, and nobody is notified.

Go to My Data

Sign in with the GitHub account that owns the repository, then choose what to remove.

Your options

OptionWhat is removedYour account
Delete one projectThat project's reports, scores, badges, ranking history, queued evaluations, appeals, saved report files and cached images.Kept
Delete all my projectsThe same, for every project on your account. Runs in the background with a progress bar.Kept
Delete my accountEvery project as above, plus your account, Spark upvotes, appeals and queue history. You are signed out everywhere.Deleted

Before deleting, you can download everything we store about you from the same page.

What happens afterwards

  • Removal is immediate, and applies to LetsVibe's backups at the same moment, so your data does not linger in older copies. Rankings are recalculated so other projects move up.
  • If an evaluation of your project is running at that moment, its result is discarded and never published.
  • Nobody else can re-submit your repository, including the site operator: only its owner's GitHub account can submit it.
  • You can come back and submit again whenever you like.

Can't sign in?

If you have lost access to the GitHub account that owns a listed repository, or you believe a listing should not be there, use the in-app Chat / Support widget (bottom-right) or open an issue on GitHub with the repository link. We will verify and remove it promptly.
