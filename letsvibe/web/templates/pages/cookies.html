Cookie policy

LetsVibe uses only what is strictly necessary to keep you signed in and remember a couple of display choices. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party cookies. You can decline storage to browse read-only, in which case sign-in and repository submission are disabled.

Third parties

LetsVibe loads no advertising scripts, fonts, images or embeds. Cloudflare delivers the site and runs a cookieless Web Analytics script for aggregate pageview counts, which sets no cookies. Ko-fi links open Ko-fi's own site, which has its own cookie policy; nothing from Ko-fi is placed on LetsVibe.

Your control

You can clear these at any time through your browser settings. If you remove the session cookie, you will simply be signed out. See the privacy policy for everything else.
